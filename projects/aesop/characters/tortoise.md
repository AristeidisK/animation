# Character — The Tortoise

**Cascade:** `brand/style-bible.md` → `projects/aesop/project.md` → this file.
**Built by:** `pipeline/build_cast.py::tortoise()` → `brand/characters/tortoise.svg`

Drawn, never generated.

---

## 1. Colour lock — from `tokens.json`

She is **`olive` shell over `seafoam` skin**. Two hues, and both are load-bearing.

| Part | Token | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Shell dome | `olive` | `#6E8B4A` |
| Shell rim, shadow side | `olive-dark` / `olive-deep` | computed −10% / −18% L |
| Shell plates | `olive-light` | computed +9% L |
| Head, neck, legs, tail | `seafoam` | `#79C4C0` |
| Limb shadow, foot pads | `seafoam-dark` / `seafoam-deep` | computed |
| Eye | `ink` | `#23212B` |
| Catchlight | `bone` | `#F7F1E4` |

**Her skin is never `bone`.** It was, in the first build, and she vanished — the
backgrounds use `bone` constantly for limestone, dust and dry stone walls, and
she disappeared into every one of them. `seafoam` is the fix and it is not
negotiable.

The seafoam also does quiet work: it is the same token as the silver flash on
olive leaves, so she belongs to the landscape in a way the gold hare never does.

---

## 2. Silhouette lock

Low, wide, immovable. The exact visual opposite of the hare, and the contrast
between their two silhouettes is most of the storytelling.

- Shell a broad flattened dome, wider than tall, sitting low.
- Heavy rim beneath it — the rim is what gives her weight.
- Four short legs, all four visible, planted.
- Head forward on a short thick neck, level, never raised.
- Where the hare is a coiled diagonal, she is a horizontal. Identifiable in
  solid black.

---

## 3. Face and shell

- **Eye:** one `ink` circle with a `bone` catchlight. Same treatment as the hare
  — the cast shares an eye language.
- **Mouth:** a single soft `seafoam-deep` ellipse, barely there. Never a smile.
  She is not pleased with herself; that is the point of the character.
- **Shell plates:** five `olive-light` rounded shapes — three across the top, two
  below — each with a soft `olive-dark` inner shadow. Flat, not embossed.
- **No wrinkles, no scales, no texture.**

---

## 4. Movement

She never hurries and she never gloats. In shot 9 she passes the sleeping hare
without turning her head — the restraint *is* the character, and any animation
that has her glance, smirk or pause breaks it.

Her walk is a slight vertical bob only. No leg cycle; the legs stay planted and
the body rises and falls. At this scale a leg cycle reads as jitter.

---

## 5. Never

- Never `bone` skin
- Never with a raised head or an open mouth
- Never reacting to the hare
- Never in the frame story
- Never plum
