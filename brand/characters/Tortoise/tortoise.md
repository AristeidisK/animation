# Character — The Tortoise

**Cascade:** `brand/style-bible.md` → `projects/aesop/project.md` → this file.
**Reference:** matches `../Owl/Owl - 01.png` and `Owl - 03.png` in construction,
shading and eye treatment. They are siblings and must read as one cast.

She is the visual opposite of the hare and that contrast carries the story: he
is a tall vertical coil, she is a low horizontal. Their two silhouettes should
be tellable apart in solid black at thumbnail size.

---

## Shared house style — identical to the Owl

- Rounded **squircle** construction — soft rounded rectangles, never plain ovals.
- **No outlines anywhere.** Shapes read by colour contrast alone.
- **Flat two-tone shading only:** one base colour plus a single slightly darker
  flat panel down the right side. Restrained. No gradients, no modelling.
- **One small white catchlight** per eye, upper-right of the pupil.
- **Thick short dark brows** floating above the eyes with a clear gap.
- Square 1:1, character centred, flat solid background.

**Difference from Kouki:** golden eye discs are hers alone. The tortoise has
large dark eyes set straight on the skin colour.

## Colour lock

| Part | Token | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Shell dome | `olive` | `#6E8B4A` |
| Shell plates | `olive-light` | computed +9% L |
| Right-side shadow panel, shell rim | `olive-dark` / `olive-deep` | computed |
| Head, neck, legs, tail | `seafoam` | `#79C4C0` |
| Eyes, brows | `ink` | `#23212B` |
| Catchlight | white | — |
| Mouth | `seafoam-deep` | computed |

**Her skin is never `bone`.** Backgrounds use bone constantly for limestone,
dust and dry stone walls, and she disappeared into every one of them in the
first build. `seafoam` is the fix and it is not negotiable — it also happens to
be the olive-leaf silver, so she belongs to the landscape in a way the gold hare
deliberately does not.

## Prompt

```
A stylised tortoise character for a preschool animated television series. Flat
vector illustration, square format, one character centred on a plain solid
background. No outlines anywhere — every shape reads by colour contrast alone.

Body: a broad low rounded shell dome in muted olive green (#6E8B4A), wider than
it is tall, with a soft squircle silhouette. Five simple lighter olive rounded
plate shapes across the shell — three along the top, two below — flat, not
embossed. A slightly darker olive rim beneath the shell giving it weight.

Skin: pale blue-green (#79C4C0). A rounded head on a short thick neck reaching
forward to the right, level and unhurried. Four short rounded legs planted
firmly. A small blunt tail.

Face: two large circular dark charcoal eyes (#23212B), each with one small white
catchlight in the upper right. Two thick short dark eyebrow strokes floating
above with a clear gap, level and calm. A tiny soft mouth line, no smile.

Shading: flat two-tone only — the olive base plus a single slightly darker flat
panel down the right side of the shell. No gradients, no texture, no rendering.

Standing on flat ground. Low, wide, immovable, patient and entirely unbothered.
Clean flat background, generous negative space.
```

## If it goes wrong

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sea turtle with flippers | Say "land tortoise, four short stumpy legs, never flippers" |
| Shell too detailed | Negative `hexagonal shell pattern, scutes, scales`; insist "five simple flat rounded plates" |
| Reads too tall | "Broad and LOW, wider than it is tall" — she must not be a dome on legs |
| Smiling | Remove any mouth adjective; "a tiny soft mouth line, no smile". Her restraint is the character |
| Skin too pale | Never bone or white. "Pale blue-green, clearly distinct from the shell" |
