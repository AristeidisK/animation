## Role

The far edge of the world. One flat silhouette, no interior detail of any kind —
this is the plane that establishes distance purely by being empty.

`road.md:22` locks the far hills as "flat silhouette, no interior detail, same
profile across all three plates". The same discipline applies here: the moment a
tree or a wall appears on this plane it stops reading as far away and collapses
into the mid ground.

Runs the full canvas width, edge to edge. It is never a floating island.

## Colour lock

| Part | Token | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Entire silhouette | `storm` | `#8B93A0` |

**One colour. One shape.** No second tone, no shading, no highlight. `storm` is
the cold blue-grey that reads as atmospheric distance against the warm greens of
the valley in front of it — it is doing the work that aerial perspective does in
a painted background, and it only works if it stays absolutely flat.

## Prompt

```
A single continuous low hill range as one flat silhouette in muted blue-grey
#8B93A0. Absolutely no interior detail, no trees, no texture, no shading, no
second colour — one solid shape only. Three or four soft rounded summits of
unequal height, the lowest to the left. Runs the full width of the frame, edge
to edge, with a flat straight bottom.
```

## If it goes wrong

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Trees or detail appear on the hills | "One solid shape only, absolutely no interior detail" — repeat it twice in the subject |
| Two or more tones | Restate "one solid shape, one colour, no shading" and strengthen the negative |
| Reads as mountains | "Low, soft, rounded summits" — archaic Greek hills, never alpine peaks |
| Sits as an island in frame | "Full width, edge to edge, flat straight bottom" |
| Too dark, jumps forward | Must be `storm` #8B93A0 exactly; anything darker reads as mid ground |
